## What actually blocked revenue (for the record)

1. **Send-button minutes.** Every outbound message required owner action;
   drafted follow-ups and preview links were never confirmed sent. A
   sales pipeline stalls at exactly the speed of its slowest required human.
2. **Response economics.** 15 first-touches → 2 replies → 0 interested.
   Real cold outreach often needs 50–100+ touches for early conversions;
   the pipeline never got the volume its math needed.
3. **The 18+ payment rail** (Gumroad/kit path) was never set up, closing
   the one channel that didn't depend on replies.
