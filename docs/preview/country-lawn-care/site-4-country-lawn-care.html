6-Step Fertilization & Weed Control

Our signature program: a fertilizer plan designed to your lawn's specific needs, with weed control built into every step of the season.

Soil Amendments

Healthy grass starts underground. We amend your soil so your lawn has what it needs to actually thrive, not just green up for a week.

Soil Moisture Barrier

Cut water waste and help your lawn hold moisture longer — a real difference during Utah County's hot, dry summers.

Flower Bed Maintenance

We make sure the only thing in your flower beds is what you planted — beds kept clean, edged, and weed-free.

Stop weeds before they start. Pre-emergent treatment keeps flowerbed weeds from coming back season after season.

Aeration

Fungus Treatments

Brown patches and thinning spots? We treat lawn fungus from the inside out and help your grass recover.
