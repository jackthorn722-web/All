Hour 44 · The fine print, out loud

Asked, answered, in writing.

What exactly does $249 include?

Design and build of a one-page site: your services, photos, reviews, hours, service area, a map, and a tap-to-call button. Copywriting included. Two revision rounds, connecting your domain, search-engine-friendly structure, local-business structured data, a sitemap, Search Console setup, and guidance for your Google Business Profile.

Is a custom domain included?

The $249 covers connecting one. The domain itself is bought from a registrar at cost — typically $12–$20/year, registered in your name, renewed yearly with the registrar. We add zero markup and you can start on a free web address if you'd rather not buy one yet.

Who owns the site and domain?

You do. The domain is registered in your name, and the site files are yours on delivery. Any developer can take over from us at any time.

What does "free hosting" actually mean?

Your site is a small, fast static page hosted on platforms whose standing free tiers are built for exactly this. No monthly fee from us or from them for a site this size. If a platform ever changed its terms, the site is portable — we'd help you move it, and your only possible cost remains the domain renewal.

What do future edits cost?

Small changes (new photos, prices, hours) are $20 flat per request, or $15/month for an on-call plan with 48-hour turnaround. No contract either way — and since you own the files, you're never locked in.

What counts as one revision round?

One consolidated list of changes — as many items as you like, sent together. Two rounds are included, which covers nearly every build we've scoped.

What if I don't like the draft?

You say "no thanks," pay nothing, and we delete the draft. That's the whole procedure. The free draft is the pitch — we only get paid when it's good enough to go live.

What happens if SiteSprint stops operating?

Nothing happens to your site. It doesn't run on us — it's yours, hosted independently, with a plain-language handoff note delivered with every build so any developer can maintain it.

Will my site rank on Google?

Honest answer: no one can promise rankings, including us. What we deliver is a strong, fast page for Google to index — clean structure, local metadata, a sitemap, Search Console setup — plus guidance on your Google Business Profile, which does most of the heavy lifting for local searches.
