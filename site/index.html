Fair questions

Why is it so much cheaper than a web agency?

No office, no salespeople, no bloated packages. We're a small student-run studio using modern professional tooling that builds in hours what used to take weeks. One page is all most local businesses need — so that's all we sell.

I already have a Facebook page. Isn't that enough?

Facebook is great — we'll link to it. But many of your customers aren't on Facebook, and Google rarely surfaces FB pages well. A real website gives every searcher a place to land and a button to call you.

What do you need from me?

About 10 minutes: business name, phone, services, a few photos of your work, your best reviews, and your hours. We handle everything else.

Are there really no monthly fees?

Really. Hosting is free forever. The only recurring cost that can exist is your domain name (~$12/yr, paid to the registrar, at cost). Optional: $15/mo if you want us on call for updates — most customers don't need it.
