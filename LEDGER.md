## Experiments & lessons

| Date | Experiment | Result | Lesson |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-07-11 | Model selection: productized 48h local-business sites vs. templates, freelance platforms, physical services | Chosen (see README) | Payment rails, not skills, are the binding constraint at 17 — pick models where a one-time guardian setup unblocks everything. |
| 2026-07-11 | Lead sourcing: raw trade searches vs. Facebook-domain-filtered searches | Raw searches surfaced businesses that already have sites; FB-filtered searches found the website-less ones | Search where the underserved businesses live, not where their competitors advertise. |
| 2026-07-11 | Per-lead verification before outreach | 10 of 21 candidates excluded (9 had websites, 1 closed) | Nearly half of "no-website" candidates actually have sites — skipping verification would have made ~50% of outreach embarrassing and untrue. Always verify. |
