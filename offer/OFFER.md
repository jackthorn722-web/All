# The Offer

## One-liner

**SiteSprint: a professional one-page website for your business, live in 48
hours, $249 flat. No monthly fees. You don't lift a finger.**

## Who it's for

Local service businesses (plumbers, landscapers, detailers, cleaners, movers,
barbers, tutors, pet groomers, handymen) that have:
- no website at all (Facebook page only), or
- a visibly broken/ancient site (not mobile-friendly, dead links, 2010 design).

These businesses lose real money daily: customers who Google them and find
nothing — or something embarrassing — call the competitor instead.

## What they get ($249, pay on delivery)

- A clean, fast, mobile-first one-page site: services, photos, service area,
  reviews, hours, and a tap-to-call button front and center.
- Live in 48 hours from when they answer 8 intake questions (10 minutes).
- Free hosting forever. Their own domain connected if they have one, or we
  register one at cost (~$12/yr, only fee that ever recurs).
- Two rounds of revisions included.
- **Guarantee: pay only when it's live and you like it.** Removes all buyer
  risk, and costs us nothing because fulfillment cost is ~$0.

## Pricing logic

- Market rate for the same deliverable: $900–$1,600 (2025–26 small-business
  website pricing guides). $249 is an easy yes, not a negotiation.
- Pay-on-delivery beats deposits for a first-time seller with no reviews.
- Upsell (offered only after delivery): $15/mo "SiteCare" — updates on request,
  within 48h. Recurring revenue, near-zero effort.

## Positioning / voice

Honest, local, fast. We are NOT an agency with account managers and $3k
invoices. Copy never claims experience we don't have — the guarantee and the
48-hour demo do the persuading. If asked who's behind it: a local student-run
web studio, sites built with professional AI tooling, payments handled through
a parent-owned business account. All true.

## Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "I don't need a website, I get work from word of mouth." | "Word of mouth still ends with a Google search — when they can't find you, that referral calls someone else. This just catches the work you've already earned." |
| "How are you this cheap? What's the catch?" | "No office, no salespeople, and modern tools that build in hours what used to take weeks. The catch is it's one page — which is all most local businesses need." |
| "I already have a Facebook page." | "Great — we link to it. But half your customers aren't on Facebook, and Google barely shows FB pages. This gives them a place to land and a button to call you." |
| "I'll think about it." | "No problem. Want me to build the draft anyway? Costs you nothing to look — you only pay if you want it live." (Draft-first close: our cost is ~0, conversion is high once they see their own business looking good.) |

## Intake form (8 questions, sent after a yes)

1. Business name and what you do
2. Phone number and email customers should use
3. Towns/areas you serve
4. Top 3–5 services (rough prices optional)
5. 3–8 photos of your work (phone photos fine)
6. Best 2–3 reviews you've gotten (screenshots fine)
7. Hours
8. Anything you definitely want on or off the site
